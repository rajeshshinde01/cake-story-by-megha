Your privacy

Privacy
policy.

A clear explanation of the information involved when you enquire or place an order, and how it is used with care.

Last updated · 24 August 2026

“Your celebration details belong to you. We use only what is needed to understand and fulfil your request.”
01

Information you may provide

When you request a cake, you may share your name, phone number, celebration details, delivery information and a reference image. The Cake Builder prepares this information on your device and opens WhatsApp so you can choose whether to send it.

02

How information is used

Information you send is used to respond to your enquiry, prepare your order, arrange payment and fulfil pickup or delivery. Cake Story by Megha does not sell customer information.

03

Third-party services

WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps process information according to their own privacy policies when you choose to use those services.

04

Your choice and control

You decide whether to send the message prepared by the Cake Builder. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in an enquiry or reference image.
