Ordering policy

Quotes and confirmation

Website prices are estimates based on size, flavour and design style. Your order is confirmed only after the final design, price, date and payment terms are agreed with Cake Story by Megha.

Lead time

Availability depends on design complexity and the order calendar. Please enquire as early as possible, particularly for wedding and multi-tier cakes.

Designs and colours

Reference photos are used for inspiration. As each cake is handmade, small differences in colour, flowers and decorative details are natural.

Pickup and delivery

Pickup is from Dighi, Pune. Local delivery availability and charges depend on postcode, distance, cake size and delivery time.

Changes and cancellations

Contact us promptly if plans change. Any refund or rescheduling depends on work already completed and ingredients or custom materials already purchased.
