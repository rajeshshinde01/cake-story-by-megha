01

Quotes and confirmation

Website prices are estimates based on size, flavour and design style. Your order is confirmed only after the final design, price, date and payment terms are agreed with Cake Story by Megha.

02

Lead time

Availability depends on design complexity and the order calendar. Please enquire as early as possible, particularly for wedding, fondant and multi-tier cakes.

03

Designs and colours

Reference photographs help us understand your preferred style. Because every cake is handmade, small variations in colour, flowers and decorative details are natural and make each creation unique.

04

Pickup and delivery

Pickup is from Dighi, Pune. Local delivery availability and charges depend on the postcode, distance, cake size and requested delivery time.

05

Changes and cancellations

Please contact us promptly if your plans change. Any refund or rescheduling depends on work already completed and ingredients or custom materials already purchased.
